What this mockup is. Four readings of the same table, all built from the app's own era-correct data and its own tokens — the type colours, the numeric face for numbers, the 17px leading, the species page's right-aligned sub-nav, hairlines instead of boxes, and no fills or badges anywhere.

The one thing to decide. The reference charts encode effectiveness as coloured cells. The design system forbids fills, so here the multiplier is coloured text: 2 in the increase red, ½ in the decrease blue, 0 in plain ink, and 1x left blank. Those two colours are the existing nature-direction tokens, so no new palette — but if you want the reference's green/red heat map, that is a third sanctioned reversal of “no fills” and needs your call.
