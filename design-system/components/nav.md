# Nav (global)
Horizontal text items, `font.style.label`. Active item = `color.accent.default` text (the screen's accent, see PRINCIPLES #3). Bar bg `bg.canvas`, bottom `border.hairline`. Search/filter muted at right.
**A11y:** `<nav>` + `aria-current="page"` on active; accent never the only cue (also weight 600 + `aria-current`).
**Known exception:** dark theme uses the same accent as light (owner decision 2026-10-04); contrast 3.28:1 on canvas, lowest 1.48:1 (`accent.strong` on `bg.subtle`). Recorded in tokens.json `$exceptions`; never use accent for text people must read.
**Phone (≤640px, `src/modules/nav/MobileNav.tsx`):** the three hover groups are hidden and one menu button (44px target, rightmost in the bar's top row) opens a full-screen sheet on the raised surface (`--surface-raised`, no shadow) listing every destination from `NAV_TABS` — group headings in small caps, entries as ghost rows in two columns, each ≥44px with a hairline under it. The open page is the accent's one use there, with semibold and `aria-current="page"`. A choice navigates through the same `setModule` as the desktop menu and closes the sheet; Escape and the close button close it too. Why not the groups: a tap fires mouseover, focus and click in a row, so the groups opened and shut on the same tap.
